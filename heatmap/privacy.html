Privacy Policy

Heatmap for iOS · Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what data the Heatmap app ("the app") processes. The app is provided by Connexa GmbH, Switzerland ("we").

No account, data stays on your device

The app has no user accounts. Your watchlist and settings are stored only on your device (and shared with the app's widgets on the same device). We do not run servers that receive your data.

Market data

To show quotes, charts and search results, the app requests public market data directly from Yahoo Finance (query1/query2.finance.yahoo.com). These requests contain the ticker symbols or search terms involved and, like any internet request, your IP address. Yahoo's privacy policy applies to these requests.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and financed by ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), IP address and coarse location derived from it, ad interaction data and diagnostic data to deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud.

  • Tracking permission: Personalized ads using your device's advertising identifier are only shown if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Before ads are requested, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choices. You can change them in the app under Settings › Privacy Choices.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Displaying market data is necessary to provide the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Personalized advertising is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalized ads are based on our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we do not store your data ourselves, requests regarding ad data are best directed to Google. For any question contact us via the support page.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change.
