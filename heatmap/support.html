FAQ

How are tiles sized and colored?
Tile size reflects market capitalization (or equal size, see Settings). Color shows the price change for the selected time range; the legend below the map shows the scale.

Are quotes real-time?
Quotes come from Yahoo Finance and may be delayed by 15 minutes or more depending on the exchange.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for "Heatmap" and choose Market Heatmap, Watchlist or Indices. Touch and hold the Market Heatmap widget to choose a market.

Can I trade in the app?
No. Heatmap is for information only and is not investment advice.

How do I change ad privacy choices?
In the app: Settings › Privacy Choices (EEA/UK/CH) and Settings › Tracking Permission.
